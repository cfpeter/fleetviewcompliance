## 1. The verdict, in five sentences

I would not pay for this today, and the reason is not the price. The one thing that stops me is that
the app tells me the wrong rules apply to my company: a carrier that `/check` itself calls
**Intrastate, no hazmat, no authority record** is asked for IFTA, IRP, UCR, PHMSA hazmat, operating

("12 dates are missing out of 13") — so the "what expires next" list, which is the whole product,

90-day BIT cycle lands on day 92; the same IFTA return is due Oct 31 on one screen and Nov 2 on

driver does not need, and counts disagree with their own lists. Fix "does this rule apply to me" and
the money/date items in section 2 and I would pay $60 a month for 10 trucks without a demo.
